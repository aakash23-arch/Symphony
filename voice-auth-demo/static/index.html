      Known limitations of this demo
      
    
        
  • Uses classical DSP features, not a WavLM classifier fine-tuned on ASVspoof/WaveFake.
    • 
        
  • Thresholds are hand-set from literature, not fitted on a labeled dataset — no EER/FPR benchmark implied.
    • 
        
  • Single-clip analysis, not true streaming inference.
    • 
        
  • Very short (<1s) or heavily denoised/post-processed audio won't score reliably.
